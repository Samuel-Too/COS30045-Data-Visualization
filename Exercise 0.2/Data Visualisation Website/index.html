Frequently Asked Questions

The Energy Rating Label shows the energy efficiency of an appliance using a star rating system. More stars mean greater efficiency and lower running costs within Australian standards.

You can lower consumption by choosing high star-rated appliances, turning off electronics at the wall instead of standby mode, and running high-power equipment during off-peak tariff windows.

Residential tariffs generally range from 25 to 40 cents per kilowatt-hour (kWh), varying by state, region, and electricity retailer.
